# Third Course
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Web: React, TypeScript, Vite, CSS modules. Existing Express API and root hosting configuration remain unchanged.
## Users
Dartmouth Class of 2030 students choosing a three-course term plan; supported majors are Computer Science, Engineering Sciences, Biomedical Engineering Sciences, and Engineering Physics.
## Product purpose
Set preferences, choose two course bundles, then compare verified eligible third courses. Each bundle contains a lecture and its explicitly required labs/discussions. Preserve the established green, white, and black visual identity.
## Data and academic scope
Published undergraduate offerings from Courses@Dartmouth, acquired outside the browser and bundled with retrieval dates, term/CRN identities, source links, coverage, and retained evidence. Fall 2026 is the default; Spring and Summer 2026 are historical reference. Future offerings are not inferred.
Requirements use reviewed 2026–27 matriculation-year evidence with explicit cohort/source discrepancies. Completion, potential planned coverage, remaining work, and Needs review are distinct. This is course planning support, not graduation certification or registration.
## Eligibility and ranking
Only completed courses satisfy prerequisites. Explicitly permitted concurrent corequisites can use the plan. Every meeting, including X-hours and effective dates, participates in conflict checks. Unknown prerequisites, permissions, times, and companion completeness stay Needs review. Invalid and unresolved pins remain visible.
Eligible results rank once per category: interests 40, major relevance 25, marginal verified requirement progress 25, preferred time 10. One best bundle per course, inspectable alternatives, deterministic ties. Scores are points, not percentages.
## Browser data
Versioned browser-local preferences. Migration preserves recognized preferences and resets synthetic completions/selections with an explanation. Official pins missing from a newer snapshot remain visible for correction. Reset removes both storage versions.
## Current source limitation
The captured offering feed contains widespread instructor-permission restrictions, including 968 of 1,013 Fall records. The remaining Fall primary records cannot form three distinct verified courses. The app must not manufacture a verified plan. Actual-data preview evidence therefore demonstrates Needs review; successful three-course mechanics are tested with a conspicuously labeled fixture outside the production build.
## Out of scope
AI chat, clubs, modified majors, BE concentrations, accounts, live seats, automatic approval verification, and full degree audits. Future AI integration must call the verified engine and keep keys server-side.
## Release gate
Review the desktop/mobile preview, details, constraints, and browser traces before any commit, push to BND, or deployment. The real-data verified-plan demonstration remains blocked by source restrictions.
