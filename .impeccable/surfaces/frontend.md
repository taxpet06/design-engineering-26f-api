# Surface brief: Club Finder (frontend/)

Scope: whole app (browse, profile, recommendations, shortlist, club detail). Mode: Operate.
Audience: first-years at the involvement fair, on phone or laptop, nervous and overloaded. Job: end with a short realistic shortlist. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build, sample data disclosed, must feel Dartmouth.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is the fair-day catalog you mark up by hand, not a card-grid directory. It refuses same-size cards and a gray screen with one accent; every club is a dense catalog entry and your choices are pen marks.
OWN-WORLD: Deep Dartmouth green (#00693e family) masthead and index tabs; cool newsprint ground, never cream; green-black ink; ballpoint-blue pen marks; highlighter yellow only behind matched terms; stamp red only for deadlines and conflicts. Type: Bitter (slab) for club names and headings, Archivo for UI and facts, tabular numerals for catalog numbers. Recognizable with content removed: green masthead with tab index, ruled entries with a circled catalog number at left.
STORY: A first-year sees each club's real cost in time, money and entry before committing, understands why it was suggested (highlighted reasons), circles a few, and walks away with a printable shortlist.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Green masthead with title and four standard tabs (Browse, My profile, Recommended, Saved n). Below, a sticky left filter rail (search, filters) beside a column of catalog entries: circled number, Bitter name, one-line description, flags, and a facts column (meets, hours, cost, how to join). On phones the rail collapses into a Filters disclosure under the search field and entries stack.
FORM: Marked-up catalog (index 6 of my grounded list), assigned by the roll; seed key ffd69368. Signature move: marking up. Save draws a pen circle around the number, Not for me strikes the name through, matched terms get highlighter, deadlines stamp red.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
